Complete Cruise Career Program

Built for candidates who want a complete, career-ready foundation before stepping onboard — combining professional photography training with maritime preparation and real career readiness.

The Complete Cruise Career Program brings together ~30 working days of professional photography training with dedicated Maritime / STCW training, giving candidates both the creative skillset and the documentation required to work and live at sea.

Students in Goa undergo hands-on photography training, cruise workflow simulations, guest interaction and sales practice, maritime safety training, and onboard preparation — finishing with assessments and university certification.

BENEFITS

Why Choose This Course?

  • Professional Photography Training: ~30 working days of intensive, hands-on cruise photography training covering fundamentals through professional delivery.
  • Cruise Photography Workflow: Learn how photography operates onboard — guest engagement, shooting conditions and delivery pipelines specific to the cruise environment.
  • Guest Interaction & Sales: Build the communication, posing and photography sales skills needed to succeed with guests onboard.
  • Maritime / STCW Training: Complete the mandatory safety training required for life and work at sea, delivered by qualified instructors.
  • Cruise Industry & Onboard Preparation: Understand onboard culture, workplace conduct and what to expect from life at sea before you sail.
  • University Certification & Assessment: Complete formal assessments and graduate with a Medhavi Skills University certification.
Duration: ~30 Working Days in Goa
