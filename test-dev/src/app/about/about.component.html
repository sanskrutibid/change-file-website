OUR STORY

OUR STORY – WHERE PHOTOGRAPHY MEETS THE SEA

Cruise Lens Academy was established in 2025 with a vision to create opportunities for talented photographers to build careers beyond the traditional land-based photography industry.

We recognized that many aspiring and working photographers have creativity, technical ability and passion but often lack access to structured opportunities that can take their careers to a global stage.

Cruise Lens Academy was created to bridge that gap.

Our programs combine professional photography training with cruise industry exposure, guest interaction, professional standards and career-focused preparation. Through this approach, we prepare photographers not only to improve their craft but also to understand the professional environment in which cruise photographers work.

We don't just teach photography. We prepare photographers for opportunities at sea and careers connected to the global cruise and hospitality industry.
