PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Cruise Lens Academy's Certification in Cruise Photography is a focused professional photography program designed for aspiring photographers, beginners and working photographers who want to develop the skills required for professional opportunities in cruise photography.

The 15-working-day program combines photography fundamentals, cruise photography workflow, portrait photography, professional editing, guest interaction, image presentation and photography sales fundamentals.

The program is structured around practical learning, professional evaluation and certification, helping participants build the confidence and technical skills needed for a professional cruise photography environment.
